# Property and Tenancy Maintenance Request Runbook

Use this runbook for tenant or resident questions about maintenance requests, access coordination, repair status, routine property procedures, and escalation. Local tenancy law, lease terms, emergency procedures, property-specific rules, and approved maintenance contracts remain authoritative.

## Operating principles

Route safety-critical issues immediately, capture evidence without assigning blame, protect tenant privacy, and do not make legal determinations in chat. A knowledge assistant may explain the documented process but must not invent landlord obligations, tenant liability, compensation, rent abatements, or repair deadlines.

## Intake

### 1. Identify property and unit
Capture only the property/unit information required by the approved process.

### 2. Capture the issue
Ask for a concise description, when it started, whether the issue is getting worse, and whether there is active damage.

### 3. Determine emergency status
Examples that may require immediate escalation under property policy include active fire, gas odor, major uncontrolled water leak, loss of essential service under severe conditions, exposed electrical hazard, security breach, or structural danger.

### 4. Record access constraints
Determine whether staff may enter under the approved notice/access process, whether the resident must be present, and whether pets or accessibility considerations affect entry.

## Routine maintenance categories

- plumbing and drainage
- heating/cooling
- electrical fixtures
- appliances supplied by the property
- doors/locks/windows
- common areas
- pest-control requests
- water intrusion
- cosmetic/non-urgent repair
- move-in/move-out deficiencies

## Status questions

A knowledge assistant can explain status labels and the normal workflow, but a live repair status should come from the work-order system. Never invent that a technician has been assigned, that a part has been ordered, or that a repair will happen on a specific date.

## Responsibility and charges

Explain only what the lease, published property policy, or verified work-order decision states. Questions about damage responsibility, chargebacks, legal obligations, compensation, or disputes require human review.

## Questions the knowledge assistant should answer well

- How do I submit a maintenance request?
- What counts as an emergency?
- How do I provide photos?
- What happens after a work order is created?
- How does access to my unit work?
- Where can I check status?
- Who do I contact if the problem returns?

## Questions that require escalation

- Am I legally entitled to compensation?
- Can I withhold rent?
- Who caused the damage?
- Will I be charged?
- Can the property enter without notice?
- Has a contractor definitely been assigned?
- Can this policy be waived?

## Closure

The request is complete when it is safely classified, captured through the approved work-order path, and the resident knows how status, access, escalation, and emergency handling work.
