# Expense reimbursement policy

## Overview

Acme reimburses approved business expenses under the policy limits. Employees must provide receipts and valid business purpose details.

## Approved categories

- Travel and lodging
- Meals during approved travel
- Client meetings with business purpose
- Software and equipment required for the role

## Rejections

Expense claims are rejected if they are missing receipts, lack business purpose, or exceed the approved policy limits.

## Decision guidance

The fast path is to verify the receipt, business justification, and policy limit before approving the reimbursement.
