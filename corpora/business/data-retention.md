# Data retention policy

## Overview

Acme retains business records according to the legal, security, and operational schedule. Data that is no longer needed must be deleted or archived according to the review calendar.

## Required retention periods

- Payroll and finance records: 7 years
- Employee files: 5 years after separation
- Support tickets and incident logs: 3 years
- Security logs: 1 year
- Draft and temporary customer materials: 30 days

## Exceptions

Any legal hold or active dispute overrides the standard retention schedule. Records under hold cannot be deleted or overwritten.

## Decision guidance

If a legal hold is active, the retention policy is paused until the hold is lifted. Otherwise, standard schedule rules apply.
