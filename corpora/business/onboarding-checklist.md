# Onboarding checklist

## Overview

Acme has a structured onboarding sequence for new employees. The objective is to ensure access, payroll, and manager setup are complete before the first workday.

## Required actions

- HR confirms start date and legal documents.
- IT issues equipment and access credentials.
- Manager assigns workspace and job tasks.
- Payroll and benefits are entered before day one.
- Security training is completed before system access is issued.

## Exceptions

If any item is missing, the new hire is not considered fully onboarded. The manager and HR must agree on the final access date.

## Decision guidance

If there is no security training or no equipment assignment, the onboarding flow should not be marked complete.
