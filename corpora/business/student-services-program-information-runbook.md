# Student Services, Program Information, and Administrative Support Runbook

Use this runbook for routine college, university, training-program, or school-community questions about program information, registration steps, common forms, deadlines, student services, administrative routing, and where to get authoritative help. The institution's official academic calendar, registrar records, program handbook, financial-aid rules, accessibility policy, and current published notices remain authoritative.

## Operating principles

Answer from approved institutional information, cite the source when possible, and never invent a student's record, grade, eligibility, financial status, immigration outcome, or academic decision. Separate general policy from individual-case decisions.

## Common information requests

### Program and course information
Explain published program structure, prerequisites, course descriptions, term sequence, attendance expectations, and official program contacts.

### Registration and enrollment
Explain the documented registration process, add/drop procedure, waitlist rules, and where the student checks live enrollment status.

### Forms and requests
Route students to approved forms for transcript requests, letters, program changes, withdrawals, accommodations, appeals, or other administrative actions.

### Deadlines
Use the current official calendar. If two sources show different dates, surface the conflict and route the student to the registrar rather than choosing one.

### Tuition and financial questions
Explain published fee schedules and administrative processes. Do not infer the student's balance, aid eligibility, refund amount, or sponsorship status without live authorized records.

### Student support
Provide approved contacts for academic advising, accessibility, counseling, career services, international-student support, library help, IT support, and emergency resources.

## Questions the knowledge assistant should answer well

- Where do I find the add/drop form?
- What are the published prerequisites for this course?
- How do I request a transcript?
- Which office handles program changes?
- What is the published withdrawal process?
- Where can I find accessibility support?
- What is the official program contact?

## Questions requiring live records or human decision

- Am I personally eligible to graduate?
- Why was my application rejected?
- Will my appeal be approved?
- What is my exact tuition balance?
- Will I receive a refund?
- Can a prerequisite be waived?
- What grade did I receive?
- What is the status of my study-permit or immigration case?

## High-risk and sensitive cases

Escalate mental-health emergencies, threats to safety, harassment/discrimination reports, legal complaints, immigration matters, accessibility disputes, academic-integrity allegations, and any request involving protected or confidential student information beyond the assistant's approved access.

## Closure

The interaction is complete when the student receives the correct published information, the relevant source or office, the next administrative step, and a clear escalation route when the question depends on personal records or institutional judgment.
